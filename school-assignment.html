01 / OVERVIEW

What is e-business?

More than an online shop: a connected way to run and grow a business.

E-business uses digital tools and the internet to manage business activities. It includes online sales, payments, customer service, supply chains, and the data that helps teams make better decisions. E-commerce is one part of e-business: the buying and selling of products or services online.

02 / THE BUILDING BLOCKS

Inside a digital business

A good online experience connects every step, from discovery to support.

  1. Online storefrontA website or app helps people discover, compare, and order products.
  2. Payments & deliverySecure checkout connects a purchase to reliable fulfillment.
  3. Customer experienceHelpful service supports customers before and after a sale.
  4. Data & operationsAnalytics helps a business understand demand and improve its work.
03 / DIGITAL MARKETING

Marketing with purpose

Good digital marketing pairs a clear message with the right audience and measurable goals.

  • Start with the audience. Learn their needs and questions before choosing channels.
  • Create useful content. Clear, helpful information earns attention and trust.
  • Measure what matters. Track visits, signups, sales, and returning customers to improve campaigns.
